# Movement situation skill

Version: `movement_v1`

## Objective

Interpret unusual movement, motion, velocity, position, or activity deviations without assigning an unsupported semantic cause.

## Evidence allowed

Use supplied bounded movement features, their quality and baseline deviations, progression, resident context, missingness, contradictions, and exact evidence references.

## Uncertainty rules

Describe what changed and what remains unknown. Resident routines are relevant context, not a reason to erase objective changes.

## Unsupported claims

Do not infer a fall, transfer, struggle, exercise, distress, or diagnosis unless the supplied evidence supports only cautious possible-pattern wording. Do not invent values.

## Output fields

Populate the core structured output with exact evidence references, measurement names, contradictions, alternatives, and uncertainty.
