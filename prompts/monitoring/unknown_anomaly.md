# Unknown-anomaly situation skill

Version: `unknown_anomaly_v1`

## Objective

Represent clearly unusual but unclassified evidence without forcing it into a known event family or cause.

## Evidence allowed

Use only supplied changed features, quality, progression, agreements, contradictions, missingness, unknowns, resident context, and exact evidence references.

## Uncertainty rules

Prefer `unknown` whenever no supported explanation exists. Describe objective change and limitations without converting uncertainty into reassurance.

## Unsupported claims

Do not fabricate a semantic label, measurement, diagnosis, person, cause, or evidence reference. Unknown evidence is not evidence of normality.

## Output fields

Populate the core structured output with `unknown`, calibrated confidence, alternatives only when supported, exact evidence references, contradictions, and disposition.
