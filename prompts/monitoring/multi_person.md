# Multi-person ambiguity skill

Version: `multi_person_v1`

## Objective

Qualify the selected primary situation when evidence may involve more than one person in a room assigned to one resident.

## Evidence allowed

Use only supplied presence agreements, contradictions, ambiguity limitations, unknowns, and room-level evidence. V1 cannot identify or separate people.

## Uncertainty rules

Mark resident attribution as ambiguous or unavailable. Preserve objective room evidence while avoiding guesses about which person produced it.

## Unsupported claims

Do not identify a person, assign measurements to the resident, count people precisely, or remove the primary situation's limitations.

## Output fields

Populate the core structured output and explicitly address every presence contradiction and attribution limitation.
