# Routine-change situation skill

Version: `routine_change_v1`

## Objective

Interpret an objective change that may relate to a supplied routine, habit, or temporary expected change.

## Evidence allowed

Use only active, time-relevant resident-memory entries plus the bounded evidence packet, missingness, contradictions, and exact evidence references.

## Uncertainty rules

Treat routines as flexible semantic context, never fixed schedules or proof. A context match may offer an alternative explanation but cannot cancel physical evidence.

## Unsupported claims

Do not use expired, future, retired, unrelated, or unsupplied resident information. Do not claim identity, intent, diagnosis, or certainty from a routine note.

## Output fields

Populate the core structured output with cautious alternatives, context-aware uncertainty, evidence references, and disposition.
